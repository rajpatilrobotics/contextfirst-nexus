# Synthetic Judge Packet Blueprint

## Safety and purpose

This blueprint defines a completely fictional, non-graphic adult training
record for demonstrating ContextFirst Nexus. It is not based on a real person,
organization, jurisdiction, investigation, or proceeding.

Every generated page must visibly contain:

> SYNTHETIC TRAINING RECORD - NOT A REAL PERSON, ORGANIZATION, OR CASE

The packet is source material only. It does not preload analysis results,
decide that trafficking occurred, determine victim status, establish guilt or
credibility, or provide legal advice.

## Controlled contradictions and limitations

1. D15 is an unsigned property record stating that passport XN0007421 was
   returned on 26 January 2025. D12 records Nila's later report that the
   passport was not returned. The packet does not resolve which account is
   correct.
2. D08 records a transfer labelled as wages. D07 and D12 describe deductions
   and wages as unpaid or inaccessible. Whether the transfer was available to
   Nila remains unknown.
3. D06 records arrival at Cedar House on 23 January 2025. D12 says Nila
   remembers reaching the worksite around 24 January 2025. The different
   locations and approximate memory require clarification rather than automatic
   normalization.
4. The author and completeness of D07, D09, D15, and D16 are unverified.
5. No signed receipt proves passport custody or return.
6. No interpreter booking confirmation is present.
7. The packet does not independently establish who controlled the messaging
   account used for the alleged communication.

The name `Nila Verin` is intentionally repeated, but names remain a
practitioner-review class and may require manual selection or a sensitive-term
list. The application must not claim universal name detection.
